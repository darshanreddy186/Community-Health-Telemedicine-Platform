Step 2 — Career Trajectory Feature Engineering

From the structured timeline, we compute career intelligence features:

Growth Features

role progression score
(Engineer → Senior → Lead)

promotion frequency

increase in responsibility keywords
(led, owned, architected)

Impact Features

systems built detected via NLP

scale indicators
(millions of users, distributed system, high-traffic etc.)

leadership indicators
(managed team, decision owner)

Stability Features

tenure length

short-tenure frequency

job-hop risk score

These features become numerical inputs to ML model.

Step 3 — ML Model Training (Career Intelligence Model)

We train a supervised model:

Model Used

Sentence-BERT → encode experience descriptions

Feature vector + embeddings → input

XGBoost / LightGBM → final prediction model

Model learns:

high-impact vs low-impact candidate patterns

stable vs unstable career trajectory

growth vs stagnation career paths

Outputs:

Impact Density Score

Career Growth Score 

Stability Risk Score



Final Candidate Score = SkillMatchScore + ImpactDensityScore + CareerGrowthScore  − StabilityRiskPenalty
